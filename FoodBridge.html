Food is wasted in one lane. People go hungry in the next.

Surplus from a wedding hall or a hotel buffet is often thrown out within hours because nobody can collect it in time. The gap is coordination, not food.

1/3of all food produced for people is lost or wastedFAO
~1 in 7people in India is undernourishedUN SOFI report

One platform between the kitchen and the community.

Donors post surplus in under a minute. FoodBridge finds the closest verified NGO that can use it, books a volunteer pickup, and confirms delivery with a photo and a meal count.

Follow a donation

From surplus to supper in four steps.

List it

Add the food type, servings and the time it stays fresh. Photos are optional.

Get matched

We alert verified NGOs within range. The first to accept gets the pickup.

Pick up

A nearby volunteer or the NGO's own van collects it. You track it live.

See the impact

Receive the meal count, a delivery photo and a donation receipt.
